The problem

Reviews in Egypt don't happen by accident

Your happiest customers walk out the door and say nothing online. Your one bad week gets a review within the hour.

01

Email & SMS get ignored

Review request emails sit unread. Egyptian customers live on WhatsApp — everything else is background noise.

02

Replying takes real time

Writing a thoughtful reply to every review — in the right tone, in Arabic — eats into a day that's already full.

03

Generic tools miss the dialect

International review software reads Egyptian Arabic literally. It can't tell "عظمة جدا" from sarcasm — and neither can a template reply.

What Som3ah does

Five tools, one reputation engine

Everything you need to collect, answer, and grow from customer reviews — without adding another app to your morning routine.

WhatsApp-powered review generation

We reach customers where they already spend their time — no email, no SMS, no app to download.

  • Automated triggers — connect your POS or booking system to send a review request a couple of hours after each visit.
  • QR codes & NFC stands — customers tap their phone at reception and land straight on your Google profile.
  • Smart routing — happy customers go public, concerns come to you privately first.

How it works

From visit to published reply, in five steps

Once it's set up, the loop runs on its own.

1

Customer visits

Your POS or booking system marks the visit as complete.

2

WhatsApp goes out

A short, polite review request is sent automatically, a couple of hours later.

3

Review lands

It posts to Google or Facebook and appears instantly in your inbox.

4

AI drafts a reply

Amiya-Smart writes a reply that matches your brand voice and the review's tone.

5

You publish

Approve as-is or tweak it, then send — right from the dashboard.
