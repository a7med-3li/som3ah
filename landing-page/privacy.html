Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This policy explains what data Som3ah ("we", "us") collects, why we collect it, and how it is used — including data accessed through the Google Business Profile API and the Facebook Graph API. Som3ah is a reputation management tool built for Egyptian businesses to view and reply to their own customer reviews from a single dashboard.

1. What data we access

When you connect your Google Business Profile or Facebook Page to Som3ah, we access:

  • Basic business/location information (name, address, connected profile ID)
  • Reviews and ratings left by your customers on Google Maps or Facebook
  • The replies you write to those reviews, so we can publish them on your behalf
  • Your account email, used to log in to the Som3ah dashboard

2. Why we access it

We access this data for one purpose only: to show all of your reviews in a single unified inbox, and to let you publish replies to those reviews without logging into Google or Facebook separately. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or share it with data brokers or unrelated third parties.

Google user data disclosure: Som3ah's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data retrieved through the Google Business Profile API is used only to display your reviews and publish the replies you write — nothing more.

3. How your data is stored

Review content, business details, and authentication tokens are stored on servers we operate, protected with encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls limiting who can view raw data. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are only used to make the specific API calls described above.

4. Data retention & deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can disconnect your Google or Facebook connection at any time from within the Som3ah dashboard, or revoke Som3ah's access directly from your Google Account or Facebook Business settings. You can request full deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below — we will delete it within 30 days.

5. Who can see your data

Only you (and, if applicable, teammates or an agency you've explicitly granted access to within Som3ah) can see your connected reviews and replies. Som3ah staff may access data only to provide support or debug an issue you've reported.

6. Children's privacy

Som3ah is a business tool and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify connected businesses by email.
